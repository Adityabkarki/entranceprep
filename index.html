Highest marks strategy

Score more by studying in the right order

Most students lose marks because they study everything equally. This portal should push them toward the topics, papers, and review habits that create the biggest score jump first.

1. Secure easy marks

Finish repeated formulas, definitions, grammar patterns, and common numerical types before chasing rare questions.

2. Review every mistake

Every mock should produce a mistake list, weak-topic list, and next practice set.

3. Simulate exam pressure

Use timed full mocks, section pacing, and negative-time recovery practice near the exam date.
